# NextAgent Launch Video — Voiceover Script (ElevenLabs)

**Video:** `NextAgent-launch.mp4` — 28 seconds, vertical 1080×1920
**How to use:** Paste each scene's block as a separate generation in ElevenLabs, or
paste the whole script with the inline tags. ElevenLabs v3 supports the emotion/
audio tags in [brackets]. Adjust the `<break>` tags if your delivery runs long.

**Recommended voice settings:** Stability ~40%, Similarity ~80%, Style ~35% —
low stability lets the emotion tags actually act.

## THE SCRIPT

### Scene 1 — Logo (0.0–1.2)
*Big sub-drop at 0.25. Say nothing or one breath — let the logo land.*

> [whispers] NextAgent.

### Scene 2 — YOUR BUSINESS (1.2–4.0)
*Impact at 1.65. Music sets the "problem" mood.*

> [serious, slow] Running a business is a lot.
> [sighs] The Wi-Fi drops. The bookings live on paper. Nobody has time for the tech stuff.

### Scene 3 — HAS NEXTAGENT (4.0–6.8)
*Impact at 4.45 — this is the turn. Get brighter here.*

> [excited, punching every word] But now — there's NextAgent!
> [warm, confident] A small AI agency, born right here in Winnipeg.

### Scene 4 — WE COME TO YOU (6.8–9.6)
*Impact at 7.25. Route line draws on screen.*

> [proud, clear] We come to YOU.
> No office. No forms. [smiling] We just show up at your door.

### Scene 5 — THE ROUTE (9.6–14.2)
*Riser builds 8.4→9.6, then a big boom at 10.1. Keep this energetic — the map is moving.*

> [energetic, building] Thirty minutes. One visit. Free.
> We watch your day, find what's slow, [beat] and fix it with simple tech.

### Scene 6 — FLASH CUTS (14.2–17.8)
*Four ticks hit the screen words. Hit each word HARD, one per cut:*

> [fast, punchy] AI! [tick] Automation! [tick] Websites! [tick] Fair prices!

### Scene 7 — PROOF (17.6–20.6)
*Boom at 18.0, chip pops at 18.4/18.9. Sound like receipts, not bragging.*

> [confident, matter-of-fact] And we don't just talk.
> [proud] Ten-plus projects — shipped, live, working. OnlyYes. LeadSniper.
> Real products. Real users.

### Scene 8 — CTA SLAM (20.6–23.6)
*Biggest boom at 21.0. Screen goes orange. This is the scream line.*

> [loud, direct, maximum energy] Book your FREE tech checkup!
> [quieter, warm, fast follow] Last stop — you.

### Scene 9 — END CARD (23.6–28.0)
*Music softens. Land it calm and friendly.*

> [calm, warm, a little smile in the voice] NextAgent. Winnipeg's tech team.
> We come to you. [beat] See you at your door.

## TIPS
- Record each scene separately and line the audio up to the scene starts —
  the sound effects already carry the rhythm, so your voice rides on top.
- If a line runs long, cut words, not the break. Silence before an impact = punch.
- "We come to you" appears twice on purpose — say it the same way both times
  so it becomes the hook people remember.
