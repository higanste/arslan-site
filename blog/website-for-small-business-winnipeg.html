Does your Winnipeg shop really need a website?

Quick answer

Yes. Not because it's fancy, but because it's how people check you out before they walk in. A simple one-page site with your hours, location, and phone number is enough to start. It works for you 24 hours a day.

Maybe you've thought: "I get enough work from word of mouth. Why bother?" Fair question. Word of mouth is great. But here's what happens now: someone hears about you, then they search your name on their phone before they visit. If nothing comes up, some of them just move on to the next place.

Your website isn't for strangers who stumble on it. It's for the people who already heard of you and want to confirm you're real. That's most of your new customers.

What people actually look for

Nobody reads long pages. A customer wants four things in ten seconds: what you do, where you are, when you're open, and how to reach you. A clean one-page site answers all four. Photos of your work help a lot too â€” a barber's chair, a finished deck, a plate of food.

If it loads fast and looks tidy, people trust you more. If it's slow or broken, they quietly assume your shop is the same way. That's just how it goes, fair or not.
