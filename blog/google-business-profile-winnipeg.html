How to show up on Google Maps in Winnipeg

Quick answer

Google Business Profile is a free tool from Google. It's the box with your hours, photos, and reviews that shows up when someone searches your type of business nearby. Setting it up properly is the single biggest free win for a local shop.

When someone in St. Boniface searches "barber near me" or "plumber near me," Google shows a map with a few pinned results. Those pins come from Google Business Profiles. If your business doesn't have one â€” or has a messy one â€” you're invisible at the exact moment someone wants to buy.
