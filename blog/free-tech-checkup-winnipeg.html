What a free tech checkup is (and what you get)

Quick answer

A free tech checkup is a short visit — about 30 minutes — where we watch how your business runs and write down what tech is slowing you down. You get a simple list of fixes and prices. You keep that list even if you never hire us.

You run a shop, a restaurant, a salon, or a clinic. You don't have time to think about "tech strategy." But the Wi-Fi drops, the bookings live on paper, and your printer jams every second Tuesday. Sound familiar? That's exactly what a tech checkup is for.

Think of it like a mechanic looking under the hood of your car — except the car is your business, and the visit costs you nothing. Here in Winnipeg, we come to you, in person, at a time that fits your day.

What actually happens during the visit

We show up, say hi, and watch how your day goes. We don't interrupt customers or get in the way. While you work, we take notes: where time is wasted, what's confusing, what keeps breaking.

Maybe your staff re-type the same order into three places. Maybe nobody can find last month's invoices. Maybe you're missing calls because there's no online booking. We write it all down in plain English — no confusing tech words.

What you walk away with

After the visit, you get a short, clear list. Each item says three things: what the problem is, how to fix it, and roughly what it costs. Some fixes take five minutes. Some are bigger projects. You decide what's worth doing.

There's no obligation. Plenty of owners take the list, fix one or two things themselves, and that's that. We think that's a fine outcome. If you want help later, you know who to call — and we're local, not a call centre overseas.

Why is it free?

Honest answer: because it's the easiest way to meet people. We're a young Winnipeg agency, and we'd rather show you what we can do than talk about it. If a fix is a good fit, we build it. If it isn't, we say so.

It also keeps us honest. If we can't find anything worth fixing, we tell you that too — and you've lost half an hour, nothing more.
