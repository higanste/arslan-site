The 10-point tech checkup list we use (steal it)

Quick answer

This is the same list we run through on every free visit — ten questions, roughly fifteen minutes. If you'd rather do it yourself, go ahead: any "no" or "not sure" is a fix worth making.

A tech checkup sounds mysterious. It isn't. It's ten honest questions about how your business actually runs. Here's the full list, so you can use it even if you never book anything with anyone.

The list

  • Can customers find you? Google listing claimed, hours right, photos recent.
  • Do you have a website? Simple, fast on a phone, with your phone number easy to find.
  • Is your Wi-Fi solid? Reaches the till, the office, and the customer seats without dropping.
  • Are your passwords safe? Not one password everywhere; two-step sign-in on email and money accounts.
  • Are your files backed up? A second copy somewhere else, tested once.
  • Does your email look professional? Business-name addresses, not personal ones.
  • Do bookings happen without phone tag? Online booking or a system, with automatic reminders.
  • Do you get paid easily? Invoices with a pay-now link, reminders that aren't you chasing.
  • Is the daily equipment healthy? Card reader, printer, computers — tested, not assumed.
  • Where do you repeat yourself? Any task you do the same way every day is an automation candidate.

How to score yourself

Count your "not sure" answers as problems, because uncertainty is where small disasters live. Most businesses pass six or seven and stumble on two or three — usually backups, passwords, and the repeated-task question. Fix the stumbles one at a time; each one is covered by a guide on this blog, from backups to passwords to the checkup itself.

The tenth question — where you repeat yourself — is usually where the biggest hours hide. We wrote a whole piece on it: automation, explained at your kitchen table.

Why we give the list away

Because a checkup is only useful if you trust it, and trust is easier when there's nothing hidden. If you run the list and want a second pair of eyes — or you'd rather someone else just handle the fixes — that's what we're here for. Either way, you keep the list.
