How to get more Google reviews (the honest way)

Quick answer

The honest method is simple: do good work, then simply ask happy customers for a review, right when they're pleased. Make it easy with a direct link or a QR code at the counter. Never pay for reviews or buy fake ones — that can get your listing penalised.

Before someone picks your shop over the one down the street, they read your reviews. It's the new word of mouth — except it's written down and everyone can see it. The good news is that getting steady, real reviews isn't hard. It just needs a habit.

Ask at the happy moment

Timing is everything. The best moment is right after you've delivered — the fresh haircut, the fixed furnace, the perfect plate. Say something simple: "Glad it worked out! If you have 30 seconds, a Google review helps us a lot." Most happy customers say yes, because they just never think of it unless someone asks.

Then make it easy. Put a small card by your till with a QR code — that's the square barcode you scan with a phone camera — that opens your review page directly. Or text the direct link after the job. Every extra tap loses people, so give them the shortest path possible. This works even better when your Google Business Profile is fully set up first.

What NOT to do

Don't buy reviews. Don't offer a free drink for five stars. Don't ask only your friends. Google's systems catch patterns, and businesses get reviews removed or listings downgraded. Besides, Winnipeg is a word-of-mouth town — fake reviews get noticed, and that's worse than having fewer of them.

Also don't spam customers with reminder texts every week. One polite ask is fine; a follow-up a week later is fine. Five messages is pestering, and it puts people off.

Reply to every review — even the bad ones

Replying shows future customers you're present and you care. For good reviews, say thanks in a sentence. For bad ones, stay calm: acknowledge, apologise once, offer to fix it offline with a phone number. Everyone reading later judges you by your reply, not by the angry person. A calm reply often wins more trust than ten five-star reviews.

Reviews also help you show up in local searches — that's part of what we cover in our local SEO basics guide. Steady reviews, steady photos, steady hours: that's the whole recipe.
