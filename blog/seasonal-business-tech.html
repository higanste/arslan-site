Seasonal businesses: getting ready for the busy season with tech

Quick answer

Do the setup in the slow weeks, so the busy weeks run themselves: seasonal hours updated everywhere, online booking opened up, automatic reminders on, and your Wi-Fi and card machine tested before the rush — not during it.

Winter here is long, and that shapes every kind of business: the patio that opens in May, the snow-removal crew that's slammed in November, the garden centre's nine crazy weeks in spring. If your year has a "crazy season," the worst time to fix your tech is in the middle of it. The best time is right now, while you can think.

Update your hours and info everywhere

Before the season starts, check every place customers look: your Google listing, your website, your social pages. Seasonal hours, the opening date, what's different this year. Nothing burns a first impression like driving to a place that said "open" and wasn't. Add fresh photos too — a snow-covered patio in July hurts you.

If you take pre-bookings or pre-orders, open them early and say so loudly. A waitlist in April is money in June.

Let the calendar take the hits for you

During the rush, the phone is the enemy. Online booking lets customers pick their own slots — for tables, appointments, deliveries, or site visits — and confirms them without a staff member touching anything. Automatic reminders cut no-shows, and a waitlist auto-fills last-minute gaps. We explain the whole setup in our online booking guide, and the reminder piece in the salon guide — same idea, any trade.

While you're at it, let an assistant answer the repeat questions (hours, prices, parking) so your front line isn't interrupted every four minutes — see our AI FAQ guide.

Test the machinery before the storm

Quiet week checklist: Does your card machine connect reliably? Does the Wi-Fi reach the till and the patio? Does the printer work when you need labels or receipts? One bad card-reader morning in peak season costs more than a month of prevention. Our Wi-Fi fixes and 10-point checkup list are built for exactly this.

Do these four things in the slow weeks, and your busy season becomes busy income instead of busy chaos.
